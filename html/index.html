Features

Everything a campus needs to run transport well

Real-time GPS tracking

See your bus move on the map with position updates streamed straight from the vehicle.

Accurate ETAs

Arrival predictions adjust to traffic and stop delays so you leave home at the right time.

Smart notifications

Get alerted when your bus is approaching, delayed or when a route changes.

Route & stop management

Administrators map routes, stops and timings once and update everyone instantly.

Safety & trip history

Works on any device

A responsive experience that feels native on phones, tablets and campus displays.

How it works

Four steps from stop to campus

  1. Step 1

    Sign in with your college ID

    Students, drivers and admins each get a tailored view of the transport network.

  2. Step 2

    Pick your route and stop

    Save your daily route once and the dashboard opens straight to it every morning.

  3. Step 3

    Follow the bus live

    The driver app broadcasts GPS while you watch the bus approach your pickup point.

  4. Step 4

    Arrive without the wait

    Timed alerts tell you exactly when to step out — no more standing at the stop.
