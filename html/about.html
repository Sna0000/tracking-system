About

Campus transport that everyone can see

SCTTS was built for the daily reality of college commutes: crowded stops, uncertain arrival times and phone calls to the transport office. One shared live view solves all three.

For students

Follow the bus on a live map, see the ETA for your own stop and get an alert before it arrives.

For drivers

A simple trip screen that broadcasts location, marks stops covered and flags delays instantly.

For administrators

Routes, stops, vehicles and trip history in one place, with reports for the transport cell.

Our approach

Built in phases, useful from day one

  1. Phase 1

    Public website

    Home, about, schedule, contact and a simulated live tracking preview — what you are viewing now.

  2. Next

    Accounts and dashboards

    Separate views for students, drivers and administrators behind a secure login.

  3. Later

    Real GPS feed and alerts

    Live vehicle data, ETA prediction and push notifications for every stop.
